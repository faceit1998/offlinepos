OfflinePOS

Do'konlar uchun internetsiz ishlaydigan kassa dasturi (Windows) · Offline point-of-sale app for small shops

Imkoniyatlar

  • Savdo, ombor, qarzlar, smenalar va hisobotlar — hammasi kompyuterning o'zida, internetsiz.
  • Naqd, plastik va aralash (naqd + plastik) to'lov.
  • Har kuni avtomatik zaxira nusxa va bir tugmada tiklash.
  • Ixtiyoriy: zaxirani foydalanuvchining o'z Google Drive'iga saqlash.

Google Drive zaxirasi

Foydalanuvchi o'zi xohlasa, dasturda Google akkauntiga kiradi. Shundan keyin dastur bazaning zaxira nusxasini foydalanuvchining o'z Google Drive'idagi "OfflinePOS zaxira" papkasiga yuklaydi va kerak bo'lganda undan tiklaydi. Dastur faqat o'zi yaratgan fayllarni ko'radi (drive.file ruxsati).

Batafsil: Maxfiylik siyosati / Privacy Policy

About (English)

OfflinePOS is a Windows point-of-sale application that stores all shop data locally on the user's computer. Optionally, the user can sign in with Google to keep backup copies of the shop database in their own Google Drive. The app only accesses files it created itself.
